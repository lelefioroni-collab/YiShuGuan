The studio

A design studio with an art practice at its centre.

YISHUGUAN is a design and art studio based in Shenzhen. We build products, identities and images for brands that need to work in both Chinese and international contexts — and we paint.

The two halves feed each other. Studio work demands precision, deadlines and a client's brief; the painting practice keeps the eye honest and the hand trained. Clients get the benefit of both.

We are a small team by choice. You speak directly to the people doing the work — no account layer, no handover, no telephone game between the idea and the file.

Working languages: Italian, English and Chinese. Deliverables are produced natively bilingual, typeset properly in both scripts rather than translated after the fact.
